Twenty regions,
one table.

Every dish begins with a place, not a plating. We cook the way a nonna cooks for family that's driven three hours to get there.

Bella Vita was built on a simple refusal: no dish leaves the pass unless it could be recognized, and defended, by the region it comes from. Our pasta is extruded in-house through bronze dies each morning. Our olive oil is pressed within sixty hours of harvest and flown in twice a season.

We do not chase trends. We chase the version of carbonara that would make a Roman grandmother nod once, silently, which is the only review that has ever mattered here.

— Marco Ferrante, Founder

Executive Chef

Elena Marchetti

Trained under three Michelin stars in Modena before returning home to Puglia, Elena joined Bella Vita in 2016 with a single instruction to her line: cook nothing you would be embarrassed to serve your own mother.

She sources direct from eleven family producers across Italy, most of whom she still calls on Sunday mornings before service.

"A menu is a promise. I intend to keep every one of them."
